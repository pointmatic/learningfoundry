# The Anomaly Named

We can now name the case.

The song is "Never Gonna Give You Up." The performer is Rick Astley, an English singer from Newton-le-Willows, Lancashire, born in 1966 and barely twenty-one years old at the time of release. The track was recorded on New Year's Day 1987 and released by RCA Records on 27 July 1987 as the lead single from Astley's debut album, *Whenever You Need Somebody*. It was written and produced by the Stock Aitken Waterman team — Mike Stock, Matt Aitken, and Pete Waterman — operating out of their PWL studio in south London, which during the late 1980s was the dominant hit-factory in British dance-pop and the source of the production fingerprint described in the previous lesson. The accompanying music video, embedded below, was directed by Simon West. It is one of the most-viewed pieces of video content in the history of the public internet.
